6. Account Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their accounts.

After a valid account-deletion request is processed, My Combine deletes the known account data managed by its active app deletion process and the user's authentication account. We do not retain that account data merely for business convenience.

A minimal server-only deletion-safety record containing the account identifier and processing timestamps may remain temporarily while authentication deletion and data cleanup are verified and previously issued sign-in tokens expire. The record remains through a two-hour safety margin after authentication absence is first observed and is removed when the required checks are complete. If a step fails, the record remains to block access while cleanup is retried or resolved.

Records outside this account-deletion process, including any information that must be retained under a specific legal obligation or that remains in provider backups, are subject to their applicable retention rules. We do not use general business convenience as a reason to keep deleted users' personal account data.
